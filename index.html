Auburn University Licensed · VWDC Technology
Signature Capability

Vapor Wake
Detection Technology

Our Vapor Wake / Kinetic Detection K-9 program represents the pinnacle of explosive and firearms detection. Through an exclusive licensing agreement with Auburn University, Gladiator Allegiance is one of only three organizations in the world authorized to deploy this patented system.

VWDC canines detect airborne scent particles shed by individuals carrying explosives or firearms — even after the carrier has left the area. A single team can screen hundreds of people simultaneously with zero disruption. Clients include DHS/TSA, Washington D.C. Capitol Police, Amtrak, and NYPD Counter-Terrorism.
