About ISOK

Premier Tactical Training Institution

ISOK is a premier tactical training institution dedicated to forging the highest standard of excellence in tactical readiness, security, and operational effectiveness. Our Academy serves as a global leader in delivering advanced training for military, law enforcement, and security professionals.

By combining cutting-edge tactics, real-world experience, and specialized instruction, we prepare personnel to operate with precision and confidence in today's most challenging worldwide environments. We are proud to employ veterans as the driving force behind our training operations and overseas service.

At ISOK, we do more than train — we build resilience, sharpen decision-making, and cultivate leaders capable of excelling in complex, high-stakes situations. With an international reach and an unwavering commitment to excellence, ISOK stands as a trusted partner for agencies and organizations worldwide.

K-9 Training Programs

Elite Canine Training & Certification

One of the largest commercial canine facilities in the United States — staffed by seasoned instructors, trainers, and kennel technicians. Purpose-bred dogs trained from birth for the highest-demand security missions.

Detection

Vapor Wake / Kinetic Detection

Trained to detect explosives and firearms on individuals in high-traffic areas. Teams screen hundreds simultaneously, trailing scent plumes even after a carrier has departed.

Protection

Executive Protection K-9

Body-worn K-9 teams for stateside and international executive protection. Trained in deterrence, threat assessment, and rapid response for high-value clients.

Patrol

K-9 Patrol Services

Expert canine teams conducting regular patrols to deter criminal activity and ensure secure environments for law enforcement, government facilities, and commercial operations.

Narcotics

Narcotics & Contraband Detection

Specialized K-9 units for borders, cargo centers, schools, and airports. Integrated with cyber intelligence for detecting trafficking-related TPPO-coated electronic devices.

EOD

Explosive Detection

ATF-licensed facility for live explosive compound training. Canines certified for airports, transit, stadiums, and government venues — trained from birth to work in large crowds.

Specialty

Bedbug & Specialty Detection

Bedbug detection for residential, commercial, and government properties. Sex trafficking interdiction using canine detection of TPPO-coated electronics and contraband.

The Force Behind the Facility

Gladiator Allegiance LLC and IAG Protection Group are premier K-9 security companies led by Miriah "Brass" Allamong — a combat-proven handler and trainer with 20+ years in foreign conflicts alongside state, federal, and special operations teams. ISOK at Legacy Ranch serves as the primary training ground powering their world-class operations.

Gladiator Allegiance deploys Vapor Wake Detection K-9 teams that screen hundreds at stadiums, airports, and major events — detecting mobile bombers invisible to traditional screening. Together with The Sapphire Group, they also provide diplomatic protection for U.S. embassies and dignitaries worldwide. Every team trains at ISOK.

Partner Organization

War Dog Guardians

War Dog Guardians 501(c)(3) ensures the well-being of our K-9s from conception to retirement. The War to Home veteran transitional program furthers this commitment — helping veterans find purpose, stability, and employment through ISOK's facility and expertly trained service dogs.

The comprehensive adoption program screens and selects suitable homes for retired service dogs, prioritizing lifelong welfare. Retired dogs transition into medical, search & rescue, tracking, and emotional support roles — connecting K-9 veterans with the people who need them most.
